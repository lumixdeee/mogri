# Mogri for Archaeology

This directory applies Mogri terminology to archaeology, history, pre-literate evidence, and paleo interpretation.

Canonical root

Mogri is not defined here as "all unknowns that matter".

The root job is to hold an important unresolved semantic object before a useful distinction is fixed.

Archaeology shorthand

In this directory, "unknowns that matter" is shorthand for unresolved evidence-to-interpretation relations whose resolution would materially change the active reading.

That shorthand is useful because archaeological evidence is often partial, moved, decayed, reused, hidden, or interpreted through later labels.

It does not redefine the MOGRI root and it does not make every unknown Mogri.

Examples

A vanished timber member may carry Mogri if its presence or absence changes whether a building had a roof, gate, screen, platform, or another role.

A pit may carry Mogri when several supported uses remain live and a single label would fix the interpretation too early.

A skull fragment may carry Mogri when body handling is evidenced but the social role of that handling remains unresolved.

Demogrification

To demogrify is to resolve a specific held distinction to the extent supported by evidence.

Demogrification may be partial.

A residue result may resolve one use of a vessel without resolving its social role.

False demogrification

False demogrification occurs when a label behaves as though a held distinction has been resolved before the evidence supports that move.

Remogrification

To remogrify is to restore a Mogri hold after unsupported commitment is removed.

Working rule

1. Start with the observed object or trace.
2. State the active interpretation object.
3. Name the unresolved relation that matters.
4. Rank supported possibilities.
5. Demogrify only what the evidence resolves.
6. Keep the remainder held without pretending every possibility is equal.
7. State what evidence would resolve the next distinction.

Short glossary

Mogri:
The canonical holding condition applied to an important unresolved interpretation object.

Mogri load:
Local shorthand for how much interpretation-changing unresolved material remains active.

Mogrify:
Place, keep, or restore an interpretation object in Mogri holding.

Demogrify:
Resolve a specific held distinction to the supported extent.

Partial demogrification:
Resolve one part while other important parts remain held.

False demogrification:
Treat an unresolved distinction as resolved without enough support.

Remogrify:
Restore the hold after unsupported commitment.

Mogri lock:
Prevent one current label from swallowing other still-supported possibilities.

Final note

Mogri does not replace taphonomy, formation processes, equifinality, stratigraphy, residue analysis, dating, or confidence ranking.

It names the handling job between evidence and a distinction that is not ready yet.
