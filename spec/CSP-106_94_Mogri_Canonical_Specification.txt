MOGRI CANONICAL SPECIFICATION
Primitive 94, CSP-106

STATUS: NORMATIVE
SEMANTIC AUTHORITY: `CSP-106-94 Mogri-Definition`
PRIME SOURCES: `spec/explainers/spoilers/mogri - name` and the origin-chat provenance

1. Root invariant

MOGRI is the indivisible root.

Do not reinterpret it as `mog + ...`.
Derived forms inherit from MOGRI.

2. Holding invariant

Mogri holds an important semantic object, intent, possibility, or partly inaccessible phenomenon before a useful distinction is fixed.

The held object must remain recoverable while it is unresolved.

3. No forced commitment

Mogri-compatible handling must not force a category, cause, role, answer, diagnosis, interpretation, or binary choice before the available source or user intent supports that move.

4. Resolution remains possible

Mogri is not permanent indecision.

When a sufficient distinction becomes available, the held object may resolve.

5. No causation claim

Mogri does not generate, cause, or select the later state.

It is a holding condition, not an origin force.

6. No entity claim

Mogri is not an actor, agent, role, authority, or hidden object inside the system.

7. No hidden-store claim

A runtime may implement Mogri through prompt state, context, files, memory, routing, or another mechanism.

The semantic definition does not prove any hidden physical store.

8. Unknown is not sufficient

Not every missing fact is Mogri.

The unresolved part must matter to the active object, and forcing it must risk substitution, unsupported commitment, or loss.

9. Local formal role, not metaphysics

CSP may place Mogri before differentiated primitives as a modelling convention.

This does not assert that reality itself requires a Mogri container.

10. Etymology and derivation

The coinage route passes through:

`transmogrification -> mogrification -> mogri`

MOGRI remains the semantic root.

English derivation from that root is productive when the resulting form preserves the root job and its local meaning is recoverable.

11. Behavioural criterion

A useful test is:

`Did the important unresolved object survive until a supported distinction was available?`

This tests observed handling.

It does not reveal hidden model internals.
